01 — About me

Logic meets creativity.

I've been in love with computers since I was 13 — when most kids were playing games, I was curious about how they were built.

That curiosity turned into eight years of building: from my first HTML pages and school Computer Science stream, to machine-learning platforms, multi-agent AI frameworks and cross-platform mobile apps today. I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor in Computer Science at Management and Science University, holding a 3.71 CGPA and a place on the Dean's List.

What makes me different is that I sit comfortably on both sides of the screen. I'm a developer who also designs, a data person who also speaks on stage, and a leader who has chaired teams to award-winning results. I care deeply about ethical, privacy-first technology — the kind people can actually trust.
